MindScope for Research Scholars

MindScope helps research scholars assess, monitor, and reflect on their mental well-being through structured check-ins, progress tracking, and supportive insights. It brings together questionnaire responses and written reflections to help scholars understand how workload, stress, coping capacity, and mood are changing over time.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence to turn check-in patterns into an overall well-being view, while sentiment analysis reads reflection notes for emotional tone. The goal is to make personal trends easier to understand without requiring technical knowledge from the user.
